🌍 India's Linguistic Diversity

India has 22 official languages and over 1,600 dialects. Hindi is widely spoken in the north, Tamil in Tamil Nadu, Bengali in West Bengal, Kannada in Karnataka, and Malayalam in Kerala. English is the lingua franca in urban areas and among educated populations.
